Resilience, Passion, and Technical Excellence

I am a proud Pakistani, a dedicated software engineer, and someone who has never let physical challenges slow me down. As a deaf individual who is non-hearing, I recognized early on that physical limitations do not define what the mind, focus, and determination can achieve. Ever since I was a child, I’ve had a deep passion for programming, solving complex logic, and building technology.

Despite every hurdle along the way, I successfully completed my full 16 years of formal education, proving to myself and others that resilience and hard work can overcome any obstacle. Over the years, I have built strong, proven industry experience delivering custom web applications, business software, and scalable APIs for clients around the globe.

This lifelong journey has made me exceptionally self-reliant, highly disciplined, and quick to adapt to new technologies. Because I communicate seamlessly through written text, visual documentation, and clean code, I thrive in remote work environments where focus, independent problem-solving, and reliable async communication matter most. For me, every project is a chance to build something meaningful, and I am always excited to bring that same dedication to my next challenge.
